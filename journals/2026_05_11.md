# Discrete Optimization Methods
	- These solutions cannot be local because for discrete points, there is no notion of a neighborhood
	- ## Mixed-Integer Convex Program
		- Objective function $f: \mathbb{R}^{m+n} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ is convex
		- The constraint set is $\mathcal{S} = \set{(x, y) \in \mathcal{C}: y \in \mathbb{Z}^n}$ where $\mathcal{C}$ is convex
		- > This becomes a Integer Convex Program when $m = 0$
		  This becomes a Convex Program when $n =0$
		  This becomes a Mixed-Boolean Convex Programs when $y \in \set{0,1}^n$
